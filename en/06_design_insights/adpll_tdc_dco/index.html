All-digital PLL: TDC and DCO quantization noise are the same ISF bookkeeping

Prerequisites: pll_noise_budget (the five-source budget, the in-band floor SrefN2+ScpS_{ref}N^2+S_{cp}Sref​N2+Scp​, the four-step derivation of the fractional-N ΔΣ third term), varactor_tuning_supply_pushing (the voltage → frequency → integrate-to-phase pipeline KVCO2Sv/Δf2K_{VCO}^2S_v/\Delta f^2KVCO2​Sv​/Δf2), sampling_pll (the other way of kicking the divider out of the loop) | Next: clock_chain_budget, serdes_clocking_connection, exercises

What this page answers​

The budget table in pll_noise_budget has five analog noise sources: reference, PFD/charge-pump, divider, loop filter, VCO. Over the past two decades, however, the mainstream has increasingly become the ADPLL (all-digital PLL): the "PFD + charge-pump + analog loop filter" is replaced by a TDC (time-to-digital converter — a circuit that measures the time difference between two edges as an integer) plus a digital loop filter, and the VCO is replaced by a DCO (digitally controlled oscillator — tuned by a bank of switched capacitors instead of a continuous varactor voltage). This page answers:

  1. How much in-band phase noise does the TDC's finite time resolution Δtres\Delta t_{res}Δtres​ become? It is the digital stand-in for the charge-pump's ScpS_{cp}Scp​, sits in the same slot of the budget table, but its magnitude is set by "quantization" rather than by electronic noise.
  2. How much out-of-band phase noise does the DCO's finite frequency resolution Δfres\Delta f_{res}Δfres​ become? It is the digital stand-in for the VCO and follows the varactor page pipeline "frequency error → integrate to phase → 1/Δf21/\Delta f^21/Δf2".
  3. How does ΔΣ dithering shape the DCO LSB away? This is the (1−z−1)m(1-z^{-1})^m(1−z−1)m shaping already derived in pll_noise_budget, reused in a new place.
  4. How the budget equation is rewritten: Sout=(SrefN2+STDC)∣Hlp∣2+(SDCO+Svco)∣Hhp∣2S_{out}=(S_{ref}N^2+S_{TDC})\lvert H_{lp}\rvert^2+(S_{DCO}+S_{vco})\lvert H_{hp}\rvert^2Sout​=(Sref​N2+STDC​)∣Hlp​∣2+(SDCO​+Svco​)∣Hhp​∣2, and why a 10 ps TDC makes the whole in-band budget lose to an ordinary charge-pump.

Classic sources: R. B. Staszewski, J. L. Wallberg, S. Rezeq, C.-M. Hung, O. E. Eliezer, S. K. Vemulapalli, C. Fernando, K. Maggio, R. Staszewski, N. Barton, M.-C. Lee, P. Cruise, M. Entezari, K. Muhammad, and D. Leipold, "All-Digital PLL and Transmitter for Mobile Phones," IEEE J. Solid-State Circuits, vol. 39, no. 12, pp. 2278–2291, Dec. 2004; R. B. Staszewski and P. T. Balsara, All-Digital Frequency Synthesizer in Deep-Submicron CMOS, Wiley, 2006 (equation numbers in both to be verified). The derivations on this page are fully self-contained: both formulas are derived step by step from "uniform quantization error → whitening → into the same integrator", identical to the site's existing ΔΣ four steps and the varactor pipeline; all numbers are marked illustrative.

Physical intuition (conclusion first): the TDC "rounds" the phase error to the nearest Δtres\Delta t_{res}Δtres​ and rolls that die once per reference period — this is a white phase-error source sampled at fRf_RfR​, sitting exactly in the PFD's seat, low-passed by the loop and, like the reference, an in-band floor; its magnitude depends only on Δtres/TV\Delta t_{res}/T_VΔtres​/TV​ (the quantization step as a fraction of one output period), independent of NNN. The DCO rounds the control word to the nearest Δfres\Delta f_{res}Δfres​ — a white frequency-error source sitting in the VCO's seat, high-passed by the loop; frequency error must be integrated once to become phase, so it looks like the VCO's 1/f21/f^21/f2 skirt but is set by the "quantization step" rather than by device noise. Neither newcomer is "electronic noise" — both are bit counts the designer chooses. The term that ISF theory is responsible for (the DCO's own device noise Γrms2/qmax2\Gamma_{rms}^2/q_{max}^2Γrms2​/qmax2​) is unchanged; it simply gains two quantized neighbours.

Step 1: ADPLL block diagram and the three noise entry points​

Three things first, because the "no N2N^2N2" below depends entirely on them:

  • Phase in an ADPLL is counted in "VCO cycles". Every reference period adds the "cycles that should have accumulated" RRR_RRR​ (the running sum of the frequency command word, which may be fractional); a counter counts how many cycles the DCO actually ran, RVR_VRV​ (an integer); the TDC supplies the sub-cycle fraction ε=Δt/TV\varepsilon=\Delta t/T_Vε=Δt/TV​ (Δt\Delta tΔt is the time from the reference edge to the next CKV edge, TV=1/f0T_V=1/f_0TV​=1/f0​ is the DCO period). The phase error ϕE=RR−RV−ε\phi_E=R_R-R_V-\varepsilonϕE​=RR​−RV​−ε is in output cycles; times 2π2\pi2π it is rad of output phase — this is the same fact as "the error is counted in VCO cycles to begin with" in the ΔΣ third term of pll_noise_budget, so TDC noise is likewise not multiplied by N2N^2N2.
  • No charge-pump, no analog loop filter. The ScpS_{cp}Scp​ and SlfS_{lf}Slf​ terms disappear, replaced by the TDC quantization STDCS_{TDC}STDC​. The digital loop filter itself adds no noise (finite-word-length effects are a separate matter).
  • The DCO is controlled by a bank of switched unit capacitors. The frequency step of the finest bank, Δfres\Delta f_{res}Δfres​, is the "quantization step"; the DCO's device noise (tank loss, active devices) is exactly the VCO's and is still set by the ISF: Svco∝Γrms2/qmax2⋅Si/f2S_{vco}\propto\Gamma_{rms}^2/q_{max}^2\cdot S_i/f^2Svco​∝Γrms2​/qmax2​⋅Si​/f2.

Step 2: TDC quantization → in-band floor (four steps, exactly like the ΔΣ third term)​

A TDC quantizes Δt\Delta tΔt into an integer number of Δtres\Delta t_{res}Δtres​ using a chain of delay elements (typically an inverter chain). As long as the phase error is "busy" enough (fractional-N, or dithered), the quantization error can be treated as white noise. Step by step:

(i) Uniform quantization error. The quantization error ete_tet​ is uniformly distributed over ±Δtres/2\pm\Delta t_{res}/2±Δtres​/2:

σt2=Δtres212[s2].\sigma_{t}^2=\frac{\Delta t_{res}^2}{12}\qquad[\text{s}^2].σt2​=12Δtres2​​[s2].

(The same uniform-distribution formula as σe2=Δ2/12\sigma_e^2=\Delta^2/12σe2​=Δ2/12 in the ΔΣ third term, except that Δ\DeltaΔ there is in "cycles" and here it is in "seconds".)

(ii) Time error → output phase. One output period TVT_VTV​ corresponds to 2π2\pi2π rad, so

ϕTDC=2π etTV⟹σϕ2=(2π)212(ΔtresTV)2[rad2].\phi_{TDC}=2\pi\,\frac{e_t}{T_V}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sigma_\phi^2=\frac{(2\pi)^2}{12}\Big(\frac{\Delta t_{res}}{T_V}\Big)^2\qquad[\text{rad}^2].ϕTDC​=2πTV​et​​⟹σϕ2​=12(2π)2​(TV​Δtres​​)2[rad2].

This step is where the "no N2N^2N2" lives: TVT_VTV​ is the output period, so ϕTDC\phi_{TDC}ϕTDC​ is already rad of output phase. If you insist on referring it to the reference side (dividing by TR=NTVT_R=NT_VTR​=NTV​), you get ϕ/N\phi/Nϕ/N, and going back to the output requires ×N\times N×N (power ×N2\times N^2×N2) — which cancels exactly. The most common rookie budget mistake is to multiply it by N2N^2N2 once more (the same trap as step (iv) of the ΔΣ section in pll_noise_budget).

(iii) Whitening at fRf_RfR​. The TDC produces one sample per reference period; the white sequence's power σϕ2\sigma_\phi^2σϕ2​ spreads uniformly over ±fR/2\pm f_R/2±fR​/2 (two-sided bookkeeping) → density σϕ2/fR\sigma_\phi^2/f_Rσϕ2​/fR​ per Hz:

LTDC=(2π)212(ΔtresTV)21fR,STDC=2 LTDC=(2π)26(ΔtresTV)21fR\mathcal{L}_{TDC}=\frac{(2\pi)^2}{12}\Big(\frac{\Delta t_{res}}{T_V}\Big)^2\frac{1}{f_R},\qquad S_{TDC}=2\,\mathcal{L}_{TDC}=\frac{(2\pi)^2}{6}\Big(\frac{\Delta t_{res}}{T_V}\Big)^2\frac{1}{f_R}LTDC​=12(2π)2​(TV​Δtres​​)2fR​1​,STDC​=2LTDC​=6(2π)2​(TV​Δtres​​)2fR​1​

(STDCS_{TDC}STDC​ in rad2/Hz\text{rad}^2/\text{Hz}rad2/Hz, single-sided, referred to the output, before the loop). Factor-of-2 bookkeeping flag (flagged every time): the literature's customary 1/121/121/12 version is two-sided bookkeeping, which numerically equals the SSB L\mathcal{L}L (the 12\tfrac1221​ in L≈12Sϕ\mathcal{L}\approx\tfrac12S_\phiL≈21​Sϕ​ cancels the ×2\times2×2 of single-siding); this site's strict single-sided SϕS_\phiSϕ​ convention needs the ×2\times2×2 (giving 1/61/61/6). This is the same class of factor-of-2 issue as [P1] Eq.(21)'s /4 (SSB bookkeeping) vs the clean time-domain /2, and as the ΔΣ third term's 1/121/121/12 vs 1/61/61/6.

(iv) Into the loop. It sits in the PFD's seat, shares the path of SrefN2S_{ref}N^2Sref​N2 and is low-passed by the same ∣Hlp∣2\lvert H_{lp}\rvert^2∣Hlp​∣2; for f≪fnf\ll f_nf≪fn​ it is a flat floor (white, no shaping), flat all the way to fR/2f_R/2fR​/2. So the TDC is the ADPLL's "in-band floor setter" — just as the charge-pump is for the analog PLL.

Dimension check: (2π)2(2\pi)^2(2π)2 [rad²] ×(Δtres/TV)2\times(\Delta t_{res}/T_V)^2×(Δtres​/TV​)2 [s²/s² = dimensionless] ×1/fR\times1/f_R×1/fR​ [s = 1/Hz] =rad2/Hz=\text{rad}^2/\text{Hz}=rad2/Hz — checks out.

Worked example (Example 1: 10 ps and 20 ps TDCs vs the charge-pump floor)​

Example 1: f0=5f_0=5f0​=5 GHz (TV=200T_V=200TV​=200 ps), fR=50f_R=50fR​=50 MHz (N=100N=100N=100, as in Example 3 of pll_noise_budget). Find LTDC\mathcal{L}_{TDC}LTDC​ for Δtres=10\Delta t_{res}=10Δtres​=10 ps and 20 ps, compare with that page's in-band floor of −121.2-121.2−121.2 dBc/Hz, and then solve for "how fine must the TDC be to break even with the charge-pump".

Step-by-step substitution (10 ps):

  1. Quantization step as a fraction of the period: Δtres/TV=10/200=0.05\Delta t_{res}/T_V=10/200=0.05Δtres​/TV​=10/200=0.05; squared 2.5×10−32.5\times10^{-3}2.5×10−3 (dimensionless).
  2. Prefactor: (2π)2/12=39.478/12=3.290(2\pi)^2/12=39.478/12=3.290(2π)2/12=39.478/12=3.290 [rad²].

Result and interpretation: a 10 ps TDC gives a flat in-band floor of −97.8-97.8−97.8 dBc/Hz, 23.4 dB above the analog PLL's −121.2-121.2−121.2 dBc/Hz (reference×N2\times N^2×N2 + CP). Solving backwards: for LTDC\mathcal{L}_{TDC}LTDC​ to equal 7.5×10−137.5\times10^{-13}7.5×10−13 (i.e. −121.2-121.2−121.2 dBc/Hz) requires Δtres=TV12 L fR/(2π)=0.675\Delta t_{res}=T_V\sqrt{12\,\mathcal{L}\,f_R}/(2\pi)=0.675Δtres​=TV​12LfR​​/(2π)=0.675 ps — finer than a single inverter delay in an advanced process (a few ps), unreachable with a plain inverter chain. This is the main battlefield of ADPLL literature for the past decade: (a) push Δtres\Delta t_{res}Δtres​ below a picosecond with interpolation, Vernier, or GRO (gated ring oscillator) techniques; (b) DTC assistance (digital-to-time converter: first shift the reference edge by the "predicted fractional phase", so the TDC only has to measure a tiny residual → a short, fine, linear TDC suffices); (c) simply use a bang-bang PD (1-bit TDC) — the quantization error is then no longer uniform white noise but is linearized by the input jitter (the KbbK_{bb}Kbb​ of the bang-bang CDR in this site's SerDes chapter is exactly that mechanism). All three roads share one goal: bring the (Δtres/TV)2(\Delta t_{res}/T_V)^2(Δtres​/TV​)2 term above down to the same order as SrefN2S_{ref}N^2Sref​N2.

Dimension check: as above; after 10log⁡1010\log_{10}10log10​ read as dBc/Hz — checks out; the inverse formula s×[1/Hz]⋅[Hz]=s\text{s}\times\sqrt{[1/\text{Hz}]\cdot[\text{Hz}]}=\text{s}s×[1/Hz]⋅[Hz]​=s — checks out.

# -> 2.887 90.69 1.6449e-10 (sigma_t ps, sigma_phi mrad, sigma_phi^2/f_R = L_TDC)

# -> -121.2 0.675 23.4 (analog in-band floor ref×N² + CP in dBc/Hz, break-even dt_res ps, excess of the 10 ps TDC in dB)

Step 3: DCO frequency quantization → 1/Δf21/\Delta f^21/Δf2 skirt (varactor pipeline + ZOH)​

The DCO control word is updated once per reference period, and its finest bank is Δfres\Delta f_{res}Δfres​. The frequency the loop wants lies between two steps; the DCO can only take the nearest — the frequency quantization error efe_fef​ is uniform over ±Δfres/2\pm\Delta f_{res}/2±Δfres​/2. From here we follow Step 2 of the varactor page exactly, with the entry replaced from "KVCOvnK_{VCO}v_nKVCO​vn​" by "efe_fef​":

(i) Uniform frequency error. σf2=Δfres2/12\sigma_f^2=\Delta f_{res}^2/12σf2​=Δfres2​/12 [Hz²].

(ii) ZOH (zero-order hold) whitening at fRf_RfR​. The control word is constant within a reference period (held for TR=1/fRT_R=1/f_RTR​=1/fR​), so ef(t)e_f(t)ef​(t) is "a white sequence at rate fRf_RfR​ through a ZOH": the power σf2\sigma_f^2σf2​ spread over ±fR/2\pm f_R/2±fR​/2 (two-sided) gives density σf2/fR\sigma_f^2/f_Rσf2​/fR​, and the ZOH's squared magnitude response is sinc2(f/fR)\mathrm{sinc}^2(f/f_R)sinc2(f/fR​) (sinc(x)=sin⁡(πx)/(πx)\mathrm{sinc}(x)=\sin(\pi x)/(\pi x)sinc(x)=sin(πx)/(πx)):

SΔf(f)=Δfres212 1fR sinc2(ffR)[Hz2/Hz].S_{\Delta f}(f)=\frac{\Delta f_{res}^2}{12}\,\frac{1}{f_R}\,\mathrm{sinc}^2\Big(\frac{f}{f_R}\Big)\qquad[\text{Hz}^2/\text{Hz}].SΔf​(f)=12Δfres2​​fR​1​sinc2(fR​f​)[Hz2/Hz].

(iii) Frequency → phase: the same integrator. ϕ(t)=2π∫tef dt′\phi(t)=2\pi\int^t e_f\,dt'ϕ(t)=2π∫tef​dt′; in the power domain multiply by (2π)2/Δω2=1/Δf2(2\pi)^2/\Delta\omega^2=1/\Delta f^2(2π)2/Δω2=1/Δf2 (varactor page Step 2.3, the 2π2\pi2π cancels top and bottom):

LDCO(Δf)=112(ΔfresΔf)21fR sinc2(ΔffR),SDCO=2 LDCO\mathcal{L}_{DCO}(\Delta f)=\frac{1}{12}\Big(\frac{\Delta f_{res}}{\Delta f}\Big)^2\frac{1}{f_R}\,\mathrm{sinc}^2\Big(\frac{\Delta f}{f_R}\Big),\qquad S_{DCO}=2\,\mathcal{L}_{DCO}LDCO​(Δf)=121​(ΔfΔfres​​)2fR​1​sinc2(fR​Δf​),SDCO​=2LDCO​

(same factor-of-2 flag: 1/121/121/12 is the two-sided reading = the SSB number; this site's single-sided SϕS_\phiSϕ​ needs ×2\times2×2.)

(iv) Into the loop. It sits in the VCO's seat and is high-passed by ∣Hhp∣2\lvert H_{hp}\rvert^2∣Hhp​∣2 — inside fnf_nfn​ the loop corrects it away (just as it corrects the VCO's close-in drift), outside fnf_nfn​ it leaks out unchanged. Shape: for Δf≪fR\Delta f\ll f_RΔf≪fR​, sinc2≈1\mathrm{sinc}^2\approx1sinc2≈1 and LDCO∝1/Δf2\mathcal{L}_{DCO}\propto1/\Delta f^2LDCO​∝1/Δf2 — 20 dB down per decade, the same slope as the VCO's white-noise 1/f21/f^21/f2 skirt; near fR/2f_R/2fR​/2 the sinc2\mathrm{sinc}^2sinc2 pushes it down a bit further.

Dimension check: (Δfres/Δf)2(\Delta f_{res}/\Delta f)^2(Δfres​/Δf)2 [Hz²/Hz² = dimensionless] ×1/fR\times1/f_R×1/fR​ [1/Hz] ×sinc2\times\mathrm{sinc}^2×sinc2 [dimensionless] =1/Hz=1/\text{Hz}=1/Hz; phase is dimensionless (rad), so read as rad2/Hz\text{rad}^2/\text{Hz}rad2/Hz — checks out (the same unit argument as the varactor page's KVCO2Sv/Δf2K_{VCO}^2S_v/\Delta f^2KVCO2​Sv​/Δf2).

Division of labour with the ISF (the same sentence as the varactor page): Γ\GammaΓ handles "a current impulse Δq\Delta qΔq hitting the tank at a given phase → how much phase"; Δfres\Delta f_{res}Δfres​ handles "a quasi-static frequency step → how much phase after integration". Both roads converge on the same 1/Δω21/\Delta\omega^21/Δω2 integrator — that is what "the same bookkeeping" in this page's title means. The ISF has two further appearances inside a DCO: the DCO's own device noise SvcoS_{vco}Svco​ (Γrms2/qmax2\Gamma_{rms}^2/q_{max}^2Γrms2​/qmax2​, completely unchanged); and the charge kickback Δq\Delta qΔq injected into the tank when a unit capacitor switches — the phase at which it lands (Γ(ω0τ)\Gamma(\omega_0\tau)Γ(ω0​τ)) decides how much phase jump and spur the dither causes ([P1] operational definition Δϕ=ΓΔq/qmax\Delta\phi=\Gamma\Delta q/q_{max}Δϕ=ΓΔq/qmax​).

Worked example (Example 2: a DCO with Δfres=10\Delta f_{res}=10Δfres​=10 kHz)​

Example 2: f0=5f_0=5f0​=5 GHz, fR=50f_R=50fR​=50 MHz, Δfres=10\Delta f_{res}=10Δfres​=10 kHz (2 ppm). Find LDCO\mathcal{L}_{DCO}LDCO​ at Δf=0.1\Delta f=0.1Δf=0.1, 1, 10 MHz; convert to the ΔC\Delta CΔC of the finest unit capacitor (tank C=1C=1C=1 pF).

  6. Unit capacitor: f0∝1/LCf_0\propto1/\sqrt{LC}f0​∝1/LC​ → Δf0/f0=−12ΔC/C\Delta f_0/f_0=-\tfrac12\Delta C/CΔf0​/f0​=−21​ΔC/C → ΔC=2C Δfres/f0=2×10−12×104/(5×109)=4.0\Delta C=2C\,\Delta f_{res}/f_0=2\times10^{-12}\times10^4/(5\times10^9)=4.0ΔC=2CΔfres​/f0​=2×10−12×104/(5×109)=4.0 aF.

Result and interpretation: −127.8-127.8−127.8 dBc/Hz at 1 MHz is 27.8 dB below the lab_20 ring VCO's −100-100−100 dBc/Hz — a ring-DCO does not care at all; but it is 20 dB above the site's canonical LC value of −148-148−148 dBc/Hz (Example B): a good LC-DCO would be drowned by its own quantization noise. And a 4 aF unit capacitor cannot be built physically (parasitics alone are two orders of magnitude larger) — so a DCO must be ΔΣ-dithered (Step 4): a buildable, coarser unit cell (tens of aF to fF) is toggled at a fast clock to buy fine "average" resolution.

Step 4: ΔΣ dithering — shaping the DCO LSB up to high frequency​

Recipe: take a dither clock fdthf_{dth}fdth​ much faster than fRf_RfR​ (usually derived by dividing CKV, e.g. f0/8f_0/8f0​/8), and use an mmm-th-order ΔΣ modulator to turn the "desired fractional frequency" into a 0/1 sequence for the finest unit cell. The algebra is exactly that of the ΔΣ third term in pll_noise_budget, except that what is shaped is frequency and the sampling rate is fdthf_{dth}fdth​:

ef → (1−z−1)mef⟹SΔf(f)=Δfres212 1fdth[2sin⁡(πffdth)]2msinc2(ffdth),e_f\ \to\ (1-z^{-1})^m e_f\quad\Longrightarrow\quad S_{\Delta f}(f)=\frac{\Delta f_{res}^2}{12}\,\frac{1}{f_{dth}}\Big[2\sin\Big(\frac{\pi f}{f_{dth}}\Big)\Big]^{2m}\mathrm{sinc}^2\Big(\frac{f}{f_{dth}}\Big),ef​ → (1−z−1)mef​⟹SΔf​(f)=12Δfres2​​fdth​1​[2sin(fdth​πf​)]2msinc2(fdth​f​),

then through the 1/Δf21/\Delta f^21/Δf2 integrator of Step 3:

LDCO,ΔΣ(Δf)=112(ΔfresΔf)21fdth[2sin⁡(πΔffdth)]2msinc2(Δffdth).\mathcal{L}_{DCO,\Delta\Sigma}(\Delta f)=\frac{1}{12}\Big(\frac{\Delta f_{res}}{\Delta f}\Big)^2\frac{1}{f_{dth}}\Big[2\sin\Big(\frac{\pi\Delta f}{f_{dth}}\Big)\Big]^{2m}\mathrm{sinc}^2\Big(\frac{\Delta f}{f_{dth}}\Big).LDCO,ΔΣ​(Δf)=121​(ΔfΔfres​​)2fdth​1​[2sin(fdth​πΔf​)]2msinc2(fdth​Δf​).

Three effects stacked together (for Δf≪fdth\Delta f\ll f_{dth}Δf≪fdth​, 2sin⁡(πΔf/fdth)≈2πΔf/fdth2\sin(\pi\Delta f/f_{dth})\approx2\pi\Delta f/f_{dth}2sin(πΔf/fdth​)≈2πΔf/fdth​):

  • m=0m=0m=0 (just toggle faster, no shaping): the power Δfres2/12\Delta f_{res}^2/12Δfres2​/12 is spread over the wider ±fdth/2\pm f_{dth}/2±fdth​/2, gaining 10log⁡10(fdth/fR)10\log_{10}(f_{dth}/f_R)10log10​(fdth​/fR​).
  • m=1m=1m=1: the +20+20+20 dB/dec shaping exactly cancels the integrator's −20-20−20 dB/dec → L\mathcal{L}L becomes flat in-band, with magnitude ∝(Δfres/fdth)2/fdth\propto(\Delta f_{res}/f_{dth})^2/f_{dth}∝(Δfres​/fdth​)2/fdth​, far below the unshaped version.
  • m=2m=2m=2: a net +20+20+20 dB/dec climb — lower at low frequency, and at high frequency rely on ∣Hhp∣2\lvert H_{hp}\rvert^2∣Hhp​∣2? No: DCO noise enters the output high-passed, so the loop does not cut it at high frequency; the high-frequency hump of higher-order shaping leaks out unchanged and can only be tamed by the sinc and by downstream filtering. This is the key difference between DCO dither and fractional-N divider dither — the latter is a low-pass path where the loop cuts the high end; the former is a high-pass path where the loop does not. Hence the DCO side usually uses only first or second order and pushes fdthf_{dth}fdth​ high.

Dimension check: as in Step 3; the extra [2sin⁡(⋅)]2m[2\sin(\cdot)]^{2m}[2sin(⋅)]2m is dimensionless — checks out.

Connection to SerDes​

The ADPLL's output σt\sigma_tσt​ (Example 4) feeds the eye diagram and BER of serdes_clocking_connection exactly as an analog PLL's does. Two ADPLL-specific reminders: (1) the TDC floor is flat white noise all the way to fR/2f_R/2fR​/2, so under a wide BW its contribution to integrated jitter grows linearly ∝fn\propto f_n∝fn​ (less "afraid" of narrowing the BW than a 1/f21/f^21/f2 source, more afraid of widening it); (2) the latency of the digital loop (TDC conversion and digital filtering each take a few reference periods) reduces phase margin and raises peaking — the "cascaded 0.1 dB" rule of pll_noise_budget must include latency in an ADPLL (external literature, beyond this page). The bang-bang CDR in a SerDes is in fact an ADPLL with a "1-bit TDC": the BB-PD linearized gain KbbK_{bb}Kbb​ mentioned in Example 1 is that road.

Key takeaways​

  • An ADPLL replaces PFD/CP with a TDC and the VCO with a DCO; phase is counted in VCO cycles, ϕE=RR−RV−ε\phi_E=R_R-R_V-\varepsilonϕE​=RR​−RV​−ε.
  • TDC quantization = in-band white floor (PFD seat, low-pass, no N2N^2N2): LTDC=(2π)212(ΔtresTV)21fR\mathcal{L}_{TDC}=\dfrac{(2\pi)^2}{12}\Big(\dfrac{\Delta t_{res}}{T_V}\Big)^2\dfrac{1}{f_R}LTDC​=12(2π)2​(TV​Δtres​​)2fR​1​ (two-sided reading = SSB number; this site's single-sided SϕS_\phiSϕ​ needs ×2\times2×2). f0=5f_0=5f0​=5 GHz, fR=50f_R=50fR​=50 MHz: 10 ps → −97.8-97.8−97.8, 20 ps → −91.8-91.8−91.8 dBc/Hz; breaking even with the analog in-band floor of −121.2-121.2−121.2 (ref×N² + CP) needs 0.6750.6750.675 ps.
  • DCO quantization = white frequency noise through a ZOH → integrate → 1/Δf21/\Delta f^21/Δf2 skirt (VCO seat, high-pass): LDCO=112(ΔfresΔf)21fRsinc2(ΔffR)\mathcal{L}_{DCO}=\dfrac{1}{12}\Big(\dfrac{\Delta f_{res}}{\Delta f}\Big)^2\dfrac{1}{f_R}\mathrm{sinc}^2\Big(\dfrac{\Delta f}{f_R}\Big)LDCO​=121​(ΔfΔfres​​)2fR​1​sinc2(fR​Δf​); 10 kHz at 1 MHz → −127.8-127.8−127.8 dBc/Hz, −20-20−20 dB per decade; corresponds to ΔC=4\Delta C=4ΔC=4 aF (unbuildable → dither).
  • ΔΣ dither: the same (1−z−1)m(1-z^{-1})^m(1−z−1)m with the sampling rate swapped for fdthf_{dth}fdth​; m=1m=1m=1 lets the +20+20+20 shaping cancel the −20-20−20 integration → flat in-band; example: f0/8f_0/8f0​/8, m=1m=1m=1 → −178.7-178.7−178.7 dBc/Hz (−50.9-50.9−50.9 dB). The DCO path is high-pass, the loop does not cut the high-frequency hump — the key difference from fractional-N divider dither.
  • Budget: Sout=(SrefN2+STDC)∣Hlp∣2+(SDCO+Svco)∣Hhp∣2S_{out}=(S_{ref}N^2+S_{TDC})\lvert H_{lp}\rvert^2+(S_{DCO}+S_{vco})\lvert H_{hp}\rvert^2Sout​=(Sref​N2+STDC​)∣Hlp​∣2+(SDCO​+Svco​)∣Hhp​∣2; Example 4: a 10 ps TDC pushes the optimum σt\sigma_tσt​ from 259 fs to 1001 fs and narrows the BW to 0.47 MHz; only 1 ps returns to 338 fs — the raison d'être of fine TDCs / DTC assistance / BB-PDs.
  • The ISF term (Svco∝Γrms2/qmax2S_{vco}\propto\Gamma_{rms}^2/q_{max}^2Svco​∝Γrms2​/qmax2​) is unchanged, word for word, inside a DCO; the ISF additionally decides how much spur the dither switching kickback Δq\Delta qΔq causes depending on the phase at which it lands.
