Spec-Driven Design Recipe: 7 Steps from Spec to Jitter

Prerequisites: fom_limit (the FOM=173.8−10log⁡10Feff\mathrm{FOM}=173.8-10\log_{10}F_{eff}FOM=173.8−10log10​Feff​ ceiling family), tank_Q_and_energy_restoration (the three forms of QQQ, 4kT/Rp4kT/R_p4kT/Rp​), tank_swing (qmax=CVmaxq_{max}=CV_{max}qmax​=CVmax​, current/voltage-limited regimes, 4πIbiasRp\tfrac{4}{\pi}I_{bias}R_pπ4​Ibias​Rp​), white_noise_to_phase_noise ([P1] Eq.(21) and the /2 vs /4 convention) | Next: pll_noise_budget, clock_chain_budget, lab_09_design_tradeoffs

Every other design page on this site runs forward: given qmaxq_{max}qmax​, Γrms\Gamma_{rms}Γrms​, SiS_iSi​, compute L(Δf)\mathcal{L}(\Delta f)L(Δf); or it does a single-knob inversion: how much must qmaxq_{max}qmax​ grow (exercises problem 1), how high must QQQ be (fom_limit example 2). Real design work runs the other way: all you have is a spec sheet (phase noise, power, tuning range), and you must decide, in order, the topology, the tank element values, the swing and the bias current, then loop back to verify the spec and convert the result into the jitter the system asks for. This page writes that path down as a reusable recipe: 7 steps, one set of numbers carried from start to finish, each step naming the formula it uses, checking units, and stating when it fails; and at the end, iteration rules — which knob to turn when the margin is short, how many dB each turn buys, and what it costs in power.

Physical intuition (conclusion first): the three numbers on the spec sheet, L\mathcal{L}L, PPP and f0/Δff_0/\Delta ff0​/Δf, collapse into one number — the required FOM. Compare that number with the ceiling family of fom_limit and the topology is decided (the ring ceiling of 168.3 dB is a hard wall). Once the topology is fixed, an LC oscillator's L\mathcal{L}L is set by exactly four things: QQQ (how small the 4kT/Rp4kT/R_p4kT/Rp​ noise source is), qmax=CVmaxq_{max}=CV_{max}qmax​=CVmax​ (how much signal charge there is), FFF (who else besides the tank is making noise), and Γrms\Gamma_{rms}Γrms​ (the waveform shape) — precisely the four factors of [P1] Eq.(21). The recipe's job is to pin each of those four factors to an element value or a bias, then sign off with Eq.(21).

Step 1: Spec → required FOM​

Using the positive-valued convention of fom_limit step 0 (larger is better), merge the three spec numbers into the required FOM:

  • Dimension check: all three terms are log⁡10\log_{10}log10​ of dimensionless ratios (Hz/Hz\text{Hz}/\text{Hz}Hz/Hz, W/W\text{W}/\text{W}W/W, and L\mathcal{L}L normalized to a 1 Hz bandwidth) → dB ✓.

import numpy as np
f0, df, L_spec, P_max = 5e9, 1e6, -120.0, 5e-3
print(round(20*np.log10(f0/df), 2))                       # -> 73.98
print(round(10*np.log10(P_max/1e-3), 2))                  # -> 6.99
FOM_req = -L_spec + 20*np.log10(f0/df) - 10*np.log10(P_max/1e-3)
print(round(FOM_req, 2))                                  # -> 186.99 (required FOM, dB)

import numpy as np

Step 3: Tank — choose CCC, derive LLL and RpR_pRp​​

The three tank quantities have only two degrees of freedom (f0f_0f0​ ties LCLCLC together); add the process-given QQQ and everything is fixed. Choose CCC first (not LLL) — CCC enters qmax=CVmaxq_{max}=CV_{max}qmax​=CVmax​ directly and sets the varactor and parasitic fractions:

  1. Lower bound on CCC: device, varactor and routing parasitics at 5 GHz already amount to a few hundred fF; too small a CCC lets the parasitics eat the tuning range.
  2. Upper bound on CCC: larger CCC → smaller LLL, smaller Rp=Qω0LR_p=Q\omega_0LRp​=Qω0​L → more current for the same swing (Step 4).

Step 4: Swing → qmaxq_{max}qmax​, bias current, power​

Now pin the denominator of [P1] Eq.(21), qmax=CVmaxq_{max}=CV_{max}qmax​=CVmax​. The swing ceiling is set by headroom: tank_swing step 4 — the single-ended swing of a differential LC oscillator in the current-limited regime is about 4πIbiasRp\tfrac{4}{\pi}I_{bias}R_pπ4​Ibias​Rp​, capped near the supply VDDV_{DD}VDD​ (voltage-limited). The recipe's choice is to push the bias exactly to the voltage-limited boundary: any further current is wasted (the swing stops growing, L\mathcal{L}L stops falling).

Take VDD=1V_{DD}=1VDD​=1 V and Vmax=1V_{max}=1Vmax​=1 V (single-ended peak):

Step 5: Noise source — Si=F⋅4kT/RpS_i=F\cdot4kT/R_pSi​=F⋅4kT/Rp​​

The numerator of [P1] Eq.(21) needs "the total white-noise current PSD referred to the tank node". The tank loss itself contributes 4kT/Rp4kT/R_p4kT/Rp​ (tank_Q_and_energy_restoration step 3); the active core's device noise, weighted by its own Γeff\Gamma_{eff}Γeff​, is collected through the noise factor FFF as F⋅4kT/RpF\cdot4kT/R_pF⋅4kT/Rp​ (fom_limit step 3). The bound for an ideal class-B cross-coupled pair (tail noise filtered out) is F=1+γF=1+\gammaF=1+γ — the standard result of Hegazi–Sjöland–Abidi 2001 (external literature, not the site's 5 PDFs; see the end of the page). This recipe takes the short-channel γ=1\gamma=1γ=1 (more conservative than the long-channel 2/32/32/3 used for the Step 2 ceiling), so F=2F=2F=2:
