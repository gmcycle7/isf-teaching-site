Exercise 4 solution (PLL optimal loop BW)

Intuition. The PLL low-pass filters the ref and high-pass filters the VCO (spec Section 10.2).

  • If loop BW fnf_nfn​ is too small → the VCO's close-in 1/f21/f^21/f2 noise isn't suppressed by the loop, so in-band noise is dominated by the VCO → large jitter.
  • If loop BW fnf_nfn​ is too large → a large amount of ref noise (and CP noise) is let through, and the VCO high-pass corner is pushed too high, so out-of-band VCO noise is also large → large jitter.
  • The optimal fn\*f_n^\*fn\*​ sits near the crossover point of the "rising ref+CP curve" and the "falling VCO curve" — where the two shaped curves intersect near fnf_nfn​, minimizing the total integrated area.

Numerical (sweeping fnf_nfn​). Using shape_output_phase_noise and trapezoidal integration, sweep fn=104→107f_n=10^4\to10^7fn​=104→107 Hz, and find the one minimizing ∫Soutdf\int S_{out}df∫Sout​df (1 kHz→100 MHz):

Result: the optimal fn\*≈f_n^\*\approxfn\*​≈ a few hundred kHz to ~1 MHz (near the crossover of the ref white-floor curve and the VCO 1/f21/f^21/f2 curve). For this problem's parameters, the grid sweep gives fn\*≈3×105f_n^\*\approx3\times10^5fn\*​≈3×105 Hz order of magnitude (shifts with Sref,KvS_{ref},K_vSref​,Kv​). This is the core trade-off in PLL noise budgeting: loop BW is neither best maximized nor minimized — an optimum exists.

Dimension check: SoutS_{out}Sout​ is in rad²/Hz, ∫Soutdf\int S_{out}df∫Sout​df is in rad² (phase variance); σt=⋅/(2πf0)\sigma_t=\sqrt{\cdot}/(2\pi f_0)σt​=⋅​/(2πf0​) is in s ✓.

import numpy as np

Exercise 7 solution (tail-noise countermeasures, illustrative)

Marked illustrative: the following cross-coupled LC VCO tail mechanism is a qualitative teaching model; the constants/specific cnc_ncn​ depend on topology, and rigorous values require transient/adjoint extraction (see real_oscillator_topologies).

Mechanism review. The tail current source's low-frequency (including flicker) noise is upconverted by 2×2\times2× via the differential-pair switching, landing near 2ω02\omega_02ω0​; it then folds back close-in via the effective ISF's c2c_2c2​ (second harmonic) and its DC component c0c_0c0​, forming a 1/f31/f^31/f3/1/f21/f^21/f2 skirt. So "tail-noise trouble" is primarily written into the ISF's c0c_0c0​ and c2c_2c2​.

Three countermeasures (each paired with one ISF quantity):

CountermeasureWhy it works (ISF quantity)
Tail filter (add a 2ω02\omega_02ω0​ notch/large capacitor at the tail)Directly blocks tail noise near 2ω02\omega_02ω0​ in the frequency domain → equivalently reduces the energy folded back by c2c_2c2​, suppressing close-in 1/f21/f^21/f2.
Waveform symmetrization (balance upper/lower half-cycles, reduce rise/fall asymmetry)Suppresses the effective ISF's c0c_0c0​; the 1/f31/f^31/f3 corner ∝c02\propto c_0^2∝c02​ (Eq.(24)), so c0↓c_0\downarrowc0​↓ directly pushes the flicker-upconversion corner away from the carrier.
Increase tank swing / raise qmaxq_{max}qmax​L∝Γrms2/qmax2\mathcal{L}\propto\Gamma_{rms}^2/q_{max}^2L∝Γrms2​/qmax2​; qmax↑q_{max}\uparrowqmax​↑ suppresses the contribution of every source (including the tail) together (claim C3).

(A fourth measure as a supplement: use a device with lower noise and a lower 1/f1/f1/f corner for the tail, or use resistive degeneration to reduce tail gmg_mgm​ noise — equivalently reducing the in2‾\overline{i_n^2}in2​​ injected into c0,c2c_0,c_2c0​,c2​.)

Result: tail noise is best addressed with three combined measures — "tail filter to block 2ω02\omega_02ω0​," "symmetrization to suppress c0c_0c0​," and "increase qmaxq_{max}qmax​"; the corresponding quantitative knobs are c2c_2c2​ (folded-back energy), c0c_0c0​ (the 1/f31/f^31/f3 corner ∝c02\propto c_0^2∝c02​), and qmaxq_{max}qmax​ (L∝1/qmax2\mathcal{L}\propto1/q_{max}^2L∝1/qmax2​).

Exercise 8 solution (allocating a jitter budget across PLL bands)

Design back-calculation strategy. RJ sources are uncorrelated → variances (not rms values) add:

σt,tot2=σt,ref2+σt,vco2⟹σt,vco=σt,tot2−σt,ref2.\sigma_{t,\text{tot}}^2=\sigma_{t,\text{ref}}^2+\sigma_{t,\text{vco}}^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\sigma_{t,\text{vco}}=\sqrt{\sigma_{t,\text{tot}}^2-\sigma_{t,\text{ref}}^2}.σt,tot2​=σt,ref2​+σt,vco2​⟹σt,vco​=σt,tot2​−σt,ref2​​.

Step-by-step substitution (with units).

σt,vco=(300 fs)2−(180 fs)2=90000−32400 fs=57600 fs=240 fs.\sigma_{t,\text{vco}}=\sqrt{(300\ \text{fs})^2-(180\ \text{fs})^2}=\sqrt{90000-32400}\ \text{fs}=\sqrt{57600}\ \text{fs}=240\ \text{fs}.σt,vco​=(300 fs)2−(180 fs)2​=90000−32400​ fs=57600​ fs=240 fs.

Corresponding phase variance (using σϕ=2πf0 σt\sigma_\phi=2\pi f_0\,\sigma_tσϕ​=2πf0​σt​, spec formula 19 reversed):

σϕ,vco=2πf0 σt,vco=2π×1010×240×10−15=1.508×10−2 rad,\sigma_{\phi,\text{vco}}=2\pi f_0\,\sigma_{t,\text{vco}}=2\pi\times10^{10}\times240\times10^{-15}=1.508\times10^{-2}\ \text{rad},σϕ,vco​=2πf0​σt,vco​=2π×1010×240×10−15=1.508×10−2 rad,σϕ,vco2=(1.508×10−2)2=2.274×10−4 rad2.\sigma_{\phi,\text{vco}}^2=(1.508\times10^{-2})^2=2.274\times10^{-4}\ \text{rad}^2.σϕ,vco2​=(1.508×10−2)2=2.274×10−4 rad2.

Result: VCO budget σt,vco=240\sigma_{t,\text{vco}}=240σt,vco​=240 fs; corresponding σϕ,vco2≈2.27×10−4\sigma_{\phi,\text{vco}}^2\approx2.27\times10^{-4}σϕ,vco2​≈2.27×10−4 rad².

Intuition: because variances add, 180 fs + 240 fs (rms) combine to 300 fs (not 420 fs) — RJ budgets must be allocated by sum of squares. This 240 fs is exactly the integrated jitter allowed for the PLL's out-of-band VCO segment, which feeds back into loop BW and VCO spec (connects to Exercise 4's optimal BW, pll_noise_budget).

Dimension check: fs2−fs2=fs\sqrt{\text{fs}^2-\text{fs}^2}=\text{fs}fs2−fs2​=fs ✓; 2πf0 σt2\pi f_0\,\sigma_t2πf0​σt​ is rad/s⋅s=rad\text{rad/s}\cdot\text{s}=\text{rad}rad/s⋅s=rad ✓.

import numpy as np
sigma_tot, sigma_ref, f0 = 300e-15, 180e-15, 10e9
sigma_vco = np.sqrt(sigma_tot**2 - sigma_ref**2)
sigma_phi = 2*np.pi*f0*sigma_vco
print(sigma_vco*1e15, "fs ;", sigma_phi**2, "rad^2")   # -> 240.0 fs ; 2.27e-4 rad^2

  • Symmetry: the 1/f31/f^31/f3 corner ∝c02\propto c_0^2∝c02​; suppressing c0c_0c0​ is the most effective lever (Exercises 2, 7).
  • Ring vs LC: Γrms∝N−3/2\Gamma_{rms}\propto N^{-3/2}Γrms​∝N−3/2; LC wins on both Γrms\Gamma_{rms}Γrms​ (small/spread out) and qmaxq_{max}qmax​ (high Q, large swing) (Exercise 3).
  • PLL optimal BW: low-pass the ref, high-pass the VCO; fn\*f_n^\*fn\*​ sits at the curve crossover, and a minimum integrated jitter exists (Exercise 4).
  • σt→\sigma_t\toσt​→BER: bathtub QQQ function; 10−1210^{-12}10−12 requires UI/2≥7.03σtUI/2\ge7.03\sigma_tUI/2≥7.03σt​, 10−1510^{-15}10−15 requires ≥7.94σt\ge7.94\sigma_t≥7.94σt​ (Exercises 5, 6).
