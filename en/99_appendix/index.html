99 · Appendix

The math toolbox used throughout the derivations, the LTV/Floquet and Leeson appendices, the Python environment, the bilingual glossary, and the reference list — for lookup and review.

📄️Math Toolbox — Math Identities

The math tools this site's derivations use repeatedly — Fourier series/Parseval, LTV convolution, Wiener–Khinchin, dB conversion, integrator response, small-angle PM, trigonometric identities, random-walk variance — each with a short proof and the pages that use it.

📄️Rigorous LTV framework: Zadeh's time-varying transfer function and the harmonic transfer matrix

Starting from signals-and-systems convolution/LTI, we derive the LTV relation y(t)=∫h(t,τ)x dτ and Zadeh's time-varying transfer function H(f,t); we prove that a periodic LTV system maps input frequency f to f+k f0 with gain equal to the k-th Fourier coefficient cₖ of the ISF — i.e., the harmonic transfer matrix; finally we prove "the ISF is exactly the phase-output conversion vector for each harmonic," tying back to [P1] Eq.(13) and the fourier page. External mathematical framework (Zadeh 1950), not among the five source PDFs.

📄️Floquet / adjoint / PPV: the rigorous foundation of the ISF

From Floquet theory of linear systems with periodic coefficients, to the monodromy matrix and Floquet exponents, the first principal vector v1(t), the adjoint system, and the PPV — a step-by-step proof of ϕ̇=v1ᵀ(t)B(t)ξ(t), mapped onto ISF Γ/q_max. Explicitly marked as external literature, not among the five downloaded PDFs.

📄️Leeson Model Derivation and ISF Comparison

Starting from tank thermal noise, feedback, and quality factor Q, build up the Leeson empirical phase-noise model step by step, then compare it term-by-term against the ISF results of [P1] Eq.(21),(23),(24) (Q↔Γrms/qmax, empirical F vs ISF physical, 1/f³ corner), then uses [P1] Sec.III-F Eq.(28)–(29) to show how the general ISF expression reduces to the LTI special case (including the Craninckx–Steyaert 2× difference), with an embedded Leeson-vs-ISF overlay plot. Explicitly flags that Leeson 1966 is not among the five downloaded source PDFs.

📄️Autocorrelation to Wiener-Khinchin Rigorous Derivation

Using three steps — the two-time autocorrelation of the LTV phase derivative, period-averaging over absolute time, and Wiener-Khinchin — this page rigorously redoes the heuristic Eq.(19)→(20)→(21) derivation route from white_noise_to_phase_noise, letting Σcₙ²=2Γrms² fall out naturally from the period-averaged autocorrelation, and compares it item by item with the heuristic version.

📄️[P2] Appendix A Verbatim Reconciliation: The Time-Domain Autocorrelation Route and the Khinchin Route

Verbatim transcription and factor-by-factor reconciliation of [P2] Appendix A, "Relationship Between Jitter and Phase Noise" (p.802-803): the white-noise time-domain autocorrelation route Eq.(40)-(44) and the autocorrelation + Khinchin route Eq.(45)-(51), mapped step by step against Steps 3 and 4 of the jitter_kernels page, with an honest account of what each route skips and what it spells out in full, plus a Python replay of both routes landing on the same kappa^2*DeltaT.
